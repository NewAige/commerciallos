## Form title and description

- Title: New commercial loan system: Credit Review survey
- Description: We are choosing the bank's next commercial loan system, which has to be live before the current contract ends in March 2029. Before we look at any vendor, we want to hear from the people who use the system every day: what works, what gets in the way, and what matters most. This survey is anonymous and takes about 15 to 20 minutes. There are no right answers; rough estimates are fine. Loan review depends on finding the full credit story quickly. We want to know how easy it is today to sample loans, check grades, track findings and support exams.
- Thank-you message (Settings > Customize thank you message): Thank you. Your answers go straight into the requirements and the vendor demo scripts. We will share a summary of what we heard, by team, once the survey closes.
